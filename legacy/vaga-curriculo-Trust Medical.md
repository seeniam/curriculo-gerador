Front-End & Growth EngineerThe Role

We're looking for someone who can take designs live and own the full experimentation loop, from building and launching A/B tests to measuring whether they worked.

This is an implementation-first role with real engineering depth. You'll work across Webflow, Heyflow, run experiments in VWO, wire up tracking and lifecycle events (Klaviyo, BigQuery), and close the loop with reporting that actually drives decisions.

We have a strong preference for someone who can operate end-to-end. You'll have a full stack developer to collaborate with, but the less you depend on them, the better.

Time commitment: Flexible and as-needed. We'll be upfront about upcoming projects; we expect the same in return.

What You'll Do

Implementation

Translate Figma designs into pixel-perfect, responsive experiences in Webflow and Heyflow
Build and maintain pages, funnels, and interactive components across platforms
Experimentation & CRO

Set up and run A/B tests in VWO, including pricing tests, cross-sell/upsell offers, and conversion funnel experiments
Partner with head of growth to define the right metrics before a test launches, capture them correctly, and declare winners based on data
Tracking

Capture new events (e.g., marketing consent) and maintain a clean, reliable tracking layer
Instrument tracking via GTM across Google Ads and/or Google Analytics depending on the needs of each test.
Lifecycle

Instrument events via GTM and pass them correctly into Klaviyo and BigQuery
Support lifecycle triggers and cross-sell/upsell flows in Klaviyo
Analytics & Reporting

Build and maintain dashboards (from BigQuery) that show funnel performance and experiment results

Who You Are

Detail-oriented and disciplined about QA: you don't ship until it's right, and "right" includes the tracking, not just the UI
End-to-end by default: you don't hand off the measurement side of a test and hope for the best; you own the loop
A natural problem solver: unfamiliar tool, messy tracking setup, design that doesn't translate, you figure it out
A clear communicator: you flag issues proactively, propose solutions, and keep stakeholders in the loop without being prompted
Driven by evidence: you ship to learn, not just to ship; you care whether the thing you built actually worked
