About the Role
We’re building e-commerce, fintech, healthcare and AI-driven products and looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join our growing team. Collaborate with product and design to deliver high-quality, scalable features.
Own the full development lifecycle, from architecture to deployment.
Write clean, maintainable code and help define best practices.
Evaluate technical tradeoffs and propose efficient solutions.
Communicate clearly and proactively in a distributed team.

Strong skills in React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.
Experience with API integrations and cloud environments (AWS, GCP, etc.).

Excellent communication skills in English (C1).
Proactive, self-driven, and comfortable working independently.
located in Brazil
